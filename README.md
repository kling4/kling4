## What Is [Kling 4.0](https://kling4.run)?

Kling 4.0 is the newest AI video model from **Kling AI**, Kuaishou's video platform, announced on **September 28, 2026**. It is built around three upgrades: **visual realism, creative control and narrative completeness**. A single generation runs up to **30 seconds** at up to **4K**, with native stereo audio.

Most video models still hand you short clips to stitch together. Kling 4.0 lets you direct a whole scene instead: set up to **10 keyframes** to pin story beats, mix up to **15 references** (images, clips, characters and voices) to keep people and products consistent, and edit an existing video without regenerating it from scratch.

The full Kling 4.0 model launches in **October 2026**. **Kling 4.0 Flash**, a faster 720p version, has been in early access since September 28.

## What Can Kling 4.0 Do?

**30-second single-pass generation.** Generate 3–30 seconds in one go. That is long enough for a scene with a setup, a turn and a payoff, with smoother camera motion and no seams between stitched clips.

**Up to 4K, with 10-bit HDR on the way.** Output at 720p, 1080p or 4K, with 10-bit HDR coming for 1080p and 4K. Aspect ratios cover 21:9, 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 and Auto, from cinema to vertical social.

**Up to 10 keyframes.** Pin character poses, scene changes and story beats with keyframe images, and Kling 4.0 animates the motion between them. It feels closer to directing from a storyboard than rolling the dice on one prompt.

**Omni Reference with 15 inputs.** Combine up to 10 images, 5 video clips (30 seconds total) and 7 elements, including voice references, in one request. Faces, outfits, products, motion and camera moves stay consistent from shot to shot.

**Native stereo audio and lip sync.** Picture and sound are generated together, with two-channel stereo and noticeably tighter lip sync for dialogue.

**Targeted video editing.** Change expressions, actions, camera moves, style or background in an existing video (up to 5 input videos) without regenerating the whole clip, or remix a reference video's rhythm and composition with your own character or product.

**Multilingual speech and sharper on-screen text.** Dialogue in Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Indonesian and more, with accents from American, British and Indian English to Beijing, Taiwanese, Sichuan and Cantonese Chinese. On-screen text, emoji included, renders more clearly.

| Model | Max length (single pass) | Resolution | References | In-place editing | Notable strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kling 4.0** | **30s** | **Up to 4K, 10-bit HDR (coming)** | **Up to 15 multimodal + 10 keyframes** | ✅ **Yes** | **Realism + storyboard-level control** |
| Kling 4.0 Flash | 20s | 720p | Supported, limited keyframes | No | Speed and low cost |
| Seedance 2.5 (ByteDance) | 30s | Native 4K, 10-bit | Up to 50 multimodal | Yes | Long sequences + consistency |
| Veo 3.1 (Google) | ~8s | 4K-class | Limited | Limited | Cinematic quality |
| Sora (OpenAI) | Short clips | 1080p-class | Limited | No | Style |

**The key differentiator:** most models give you a clip and stop there. Kling 4.0 gives you a *directed scene*: 30 seconds in one pass, keyframes for the story beats, references for consistency and editing for the final touches, so you ship finished shots instead of fragments.

Practical advantages:

- **Direct, don't gamble** — keyframes and @-mentioned references tell the model what must happen and what must stay the same
- **One take, fewer cuts** — a single 30-second pass removes the continuity risk of stitching clips
- **Sound included** — stereo audio and lip-synced dialogue come out with the picture
- **Fix, don't re-roll** — targeted edits change one detail without regenerating the whole video

## How to Use Kling 4.0

**1. Visit the site.** Open [kling4.run](https://kling4.run/ai-video-generator). It runs in the browser with nothing to install, and creating an account is free.

**2. Pick your starting point.**

- **Text to video** — describe the scene in natural language
- **Image to video** — upload a photo of a character, product or place and describe the motion
- **Reference to video** — add images, video clips or audio and `@`-mention each one in your prompt (`@image1`, `@video1`, `@audio1`)

**3. Write a detailed prompt.** Cover the subject, action, sette, tracking), lighting (golden hour, soft window light) and
sound, and say what to take from each reference. The [Kling 4.0 prompt guide](https://kling4.run/docs/prompt-guide) has formulas and ready-to-use
examples.

**4. Choose settings and generate.** Pick the aspect ratio (16:e), length and resolution. The credit cost is shown before you
start.

**5. Download and share.** Finished videos are saved to My Videos, ready to download as MP4 for YouTube, TikTok, Reels or ads.
